FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about eye checkups, cataract, LASIK, retina and glaucoma treatment.

Talk to Hospital

A routine eye checkup is recommended if you have blurred vision, headache, eye strain, diabetes, family history of glaucoma or if you are above 40 years of age.

Common symptoms include cloudy vision, glare, difficulty seeing at night, faded colors and frequent change in glasses number.

LASIK suitability depends on eye power stability, corneal thickness, age, eye health and expectations. A detailed refractive evaluation is required before deciding.

Diabetes can damage retinal blood vessels without early symptoms. Regular screening helps detect diabetic retinopathy early and prevent vision loss.

Light flashes, new floaters, curtain-like shadow, sudden blurred vision or central vision loss should be checked urgently.
